Technical Information

This application is designed to control Mobiled and Mobiled XL devices through UDP realtime protocol. Without Mobiled or Mobiled XL devices, this app can't be used. This app is the property of Andrea Croce and was designed by Andrea Croce and Maurizio Puzzanghera.

Mobiled LED Light

The Mobiled LED light is a battery-operated portable architectural RGBW LED light that can be controlled with DMX via Artnet or sACN protocol (E1.31) and via App with Smartphone and Tablet on Android and iOS systems. It features:

  • 20W Osram Ostar RGBW LED
  • 7Ah battery pack for a minimum operation of 8 hours
  • Comes with a 55° and 120° lens. Lenses with diversified apertures up to 15° optional
  • Can mount fixing rings that allow to house optical fibers up to 10mm in diameter or whips up to 200 fibers of 0.75mm
  • Also includes hooks and accessories to mount the light on any support

Mobiled LED Light Specifications

  • LED Type: Osram Ostar RGBW
  • LED Wattage: 20W
  • Battery Capacity: 7Ah
  • Operation Time: Minimum 8 hours
  • Control Protocol: DMX via Artnet or sACN (E1.31)
  • Lens: 55° and 120° included. Diversified apertures up to 15° optional
  • Mounting: Fixing rings for optical fibers up to 10mm in diameter or whips up to 200 fibers of 0.75mm. Hooks and accessories for mounting on any support included
