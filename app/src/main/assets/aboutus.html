About MobileD

MobileD Android · Version {{APP_VERSION}}

Control MobileD and MobileD XL portable, battery-powered RGBW lights over Wi-Fi for architectural, accent and event lighting.

App features

  • Discover and configure your lights, organize groups and save or load your configuration.
  • Adjust RGBW colour and brightness, switch lights on or off and identify individual fixtures.
  • Run Legacy effects and coordinate Matrix effects across groups of lights.
  • Use Tap Sync with a visible tap flash and BPM readout for Color Change and Theater.
  • Create Music effects from supported audio playing on your Android device and enjoy them across your group of lights. Playback capture requires Android 10 or later and your authorization.

MobileD — 20 W

  • Light source: 20 W OSRAM OSTAR RGBW LED (LE RTDUW S2W).
  • Battery: 2 LiTech Power Li-ion 2S1P packs, each rated 7.2 V / 3.5 Ah.
  • Rated minimum autonomy: 10 hours.
  • Charging time: up to 4 hours.
  • Dimensions (W × H × D): 100 × 46.1 × 76 mm.
  • Weight: 0.5 kg.

MobileD XL — 40 W

  • Light source: 40 W OSRAM OSTAR RGBW LED (LE RTDUW S2WN).
  • Battery: 4 LiTech Power Li-ion 2S1P packs, each rated 7.2 V / 3.5 Ah.
  • Rated minimum autonomy: 10 hours.
  • Charging time: up to 8 hours.
  • Dimensions (W × H × D): 106 × 54 × 100 mm.
  • Weight: 1.0 kg.

Shared product features

  • Optics: 60° Fresnel lens, 40 mm diameter; 120° glass lens also included.
  • Optional accessories: optics from 10° to 90° and supports for fibre optics, tubes and diffusers.
  • Control: Wireless control with the MobileD App and DMX control.
  • DMX OUT: 3-pin female XLR connector.
  • Light control: electronic 0–100% dimming and flicker-free LED operation.
  • Body: anodized die-cast aluminium; IP20, for indoor use.
  • Charging supply: 12 V DC / 1 A, with a 5.5 × 2.1 mm connector.
  • Device setup and firmware updates: open the light's settings page.

Runtime and charging figures are manufacturer ratings from the 2026 product manual; actual runtime depends on brightness, effects and battery condition.
