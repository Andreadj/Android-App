Help and Support


  1. Connect your Smartphone or Tablet to MobileD Access Point; open your WiFi settings and connect to “MobileD_c07be9ae114c” (example of a MobileD AP name).

  2. Once connected scan for devices clicking on the “+” button

  3. Once your Device has been found you can control it clicking on it; with a long press a Captive portal will be showed to change Settings, with a short press a Controls Area will be showed to control RGBW leds in realtime or to recall special effects

User Manual download at www.mobiledonline.eu


Limited support by e-mail is available at info@mobiledonline.eu
