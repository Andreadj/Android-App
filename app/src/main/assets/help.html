Help and Support

Use MobileD Android to control your lights, create groups and save your favourite colours and effects.

Getting started

  • Connect your Android device and MobileD lights to the same Wi-Fi network, or connect directly to a light's Wi-Fi network.
  • Use the + button on Home to find and add your lights.
  • The list updates automatically while the app is open. A saved light is recognised when it reconnects.
  • Open an available light or group to enter the Controller. Unavailable lights remain saved for later use.

Devices and Groups

  • All Devices lets you control all your registered lights together.
  • Create your own groups to control selected lights together.
  • Pixel ID sets each light's position in coordinated effects. Several lights may share the same position.
  • Selecting a group includes the lights available at that moment. Select the group again to include lights that reconnect later.

Controller

  • Color provides Hue, Color Brightness and White Brightness controls, plus ten saved-colour slots in two rows of five. Tap a saved colour to recall it; press and hold a slot to save or replace it with the current colour and both brightness values.
  • Effects contains Legacy effects and their controls. Without a Master, they work independently on all selected available lights and do not require Pixel IDs.
  • To synchronise Legacy, choose one Master with Pixel ID 0. The Master must be available. Lights participate from ID 0 up to the first missing position, including lights sharing the same ID.
  • Matrix offers coordinated group effects. Choose one Master at Pixel ID 0 and assign consecutive positions starting at 0.
  • Music creates lighting effects from supported audio playing on your Android device. It requires Android 10 or later and your permission when prompted. Some media apps do not allow their audio to be shared.
  • Music does not require a Master. Assign positions starting at Pixel ID 0; positions after the first gap are excluded.

Troubleshooting

  • If a light is unavailable, check that it is powered on and connected to the same Wi-Fi network, then refresh the list.
  • If a light reconnects after you selected its group, select the group again.
  • If synchronised Legacy or Matrix cannot start, check that the chosen Master is available and has Pixel ID 0. To use Legacy independently, select No Master.
  • If a coordinated effect does not include a light, check its Pixel ID and the group's consecutive positions.
  • If Music does not start, allow audio sharing when prompted and check that the chosen media app permits it.
